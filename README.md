# Pragathi-Retail-Store-Project

This is a simple web-based Retail Store application created using Html that allows users to browse and purchase items, view prices, and place orders. The store offers a variety of products, including women's dresses, men's dresses, children's dresses, shoes, and laptops.

## Features
### View Products: 
Users can browse a wide range of products, including: <br>
Women's Dresses <br>
Men's Dresses <br>
Children's Dresses <br>
Shoes <br>
Laptops <br>

### View Price: 
Each product comes with a View Price button. When clicked, the price, quatatity and size details are displayed of the selected item.

### Add to Cart: 
When users find an item they want to purchase, they can click the Add to Cart button.
A confirmation success message will appear on the screen, notifying the user that the item has been added to the cart.

### View Cart: 
Users can click on the View Cart button to see all the items added to their cart along with their prices.
The total price of all items in the cart is displayed at the bottom.

### Place Order: 
Once the user has added items to the cart, they can click the Place Order button to proceed with placing the order.

### Contact Us:
In the Contact Us section, users can fill out a form with their contact details (e.g., name, email, message).
After submitting, an alert will confirm that the contact details have been successfully submitted.
